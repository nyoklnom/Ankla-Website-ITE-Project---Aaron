Jujutsu Kaisen Dark Myth

by Aaron

Jujutsu Kaisen Dark Myth

Chapter 1

Beneath the ordinary world existed a hidden reality where fear, hatred, grief, and regret could give birth to terrifying creatures known as Curses. These supernatural beings fed on the negative emotions of humanity, haunting abandoned places and preying upon people who could neither see nor understand them. To protect the living, a secret group of sorcerers devoted their lives to fighting these unseen horrors.

For centuries, stories of cursed objects and mysterious disappearances had circulated among ordinary people. Most dismissed them as rumors, but jujutsu sorcerers understood the truth. Some objects carried such dangerous energy that even a small mistake could unleash a disaster capable of threatening an entire city.

Yuji Itadori was an unusually athletic high school student who preferred spending time with his friends over joining the school's sports teams. Although he knew little about the supernatural world, his extraordinary physical strength would soon pull him into a conflict far beyond his understanding.

Sometimes, one decision is enough to change the fate of the entire world.

One evening, Yuji discovered that his school's occult club had come into possession of a mysterious object: a shriveled finger sealed with protective charms. The object was actually a fragment of Ryomen Sukuna, a legendary curse feared as the King of Curses. Its immense power attracted dangerous spirits that sought to consume it.

When curses attacked Yuji's friends, he refused to abandon them. With no proper training and no understanding of cursed energy, he faced a supernatural threat that should have been impossible for an ordinary student to survive. His desperation to save the people he cared about forced him to make a decision that would change his life forever.

To gain the power needed to fight back, Yuji swallowed Sukuna's cursed finger. The forbidden act allowed the ancient curse to enter his body, granting him access to extraordinary power but placing his life under the control of forces he could barely comprehend.

THE KING OF CURSES AWAKENS

Sukuna was no ordinary spirit. He possessed a cruel intelligence, terrifying strength, and a complete disregard for human life. His presence threatened everyone nearby, and his return raised a frightening question among the sorcerers: what would happen if the King of Curses regained his full power?

Yuji, however, demonstrated something unexpected. Despite becoming Sukuna's vessel, he could maintain control over his own body. This rare ability made him valuable to the jujutsu world, but it also made him dangerous in the eyes of those who feared Sukuna's eventual return.

Megumi Fushiguro, the sorcerer who had arrived to recover the cursed object, witnessed Yuji's unusual potential. Soon afterward, Satoru Gojo, an immensely powerful jujutsu sorcerer, became involved in Yuji's fate. Under Gojo's guidance, Yuji entered the world of jujutsu sorcery, where every lesson could mean the difference between survival and death.

The greatest danger was not always the curse standing in front of you, but the darkness hidden within.

At Jujutsu High, Yuji encountered other students who had chosen to fight the supernatural threats haunting humanity. Among them was Nobara Kugisaki, a confident sorcerer who wielded a hammer, nails, and a technique that connected her attacks to cursed energy. Despite their different personalities, the students learned to cooperate as they faced increasingly dangerous missions.

Their training revealed that curses came in many forms. Some were born from common fears, while others grew powerful enough to challenge experienced sorcerers. Every encounter required careful judgment, because a curse's appearance rarely revealed the full extent of its abilities.

Beyond the schools and training grounds, an older conflict was beginning to unfold. Powerful curses and mysterious enemies operated from the shadows, questioning the balance between humans and the supernatural world. Their plans threatened to expose ordinary people to horrors they were never meant to witness.

As Yuji continued his training, he struggled with the responsibility of carrying Sukuna inside him. He wanted to save others, but the power he possessed could also become a weapon of destruction. Every battle forced him to confront the possibility that protecting humanity might require sacrifices he was not prepared to make.

The myths surrounding Sukuna were more than stories from a distant past. They were warnings about what happened when extraordinary power existed without compassion or restraint. Yuji's journey would test whether a person could carry such darkness and still choose to do what was right.

With Gojo's guidance and his friends fighting beside him, Yuji stepped deeper into a world of hidden curses, forbidden techniques, and ancient grudges. The threats ahead would grow stronger, and the secrets behind the jujutsu world would become increasingly difficult to ignore.

The King of Curses had returned to the world through an unexpected vessel. As shadows gathered and ancient powers stirred, Yuji Itadori faced a destiny that could determine the fate of countless lives. His fight against the darkness had only begun, and the true meaning of the Dark Myth was waiting to be revealed.
