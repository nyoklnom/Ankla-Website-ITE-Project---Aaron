Jujutsu Kaisen

by Aaron

Jujutsu Kaisen

Chapter 1

In a world where dangerous supernatural beings known as Curses lurked in the shadows, most people lived their everyday lives without ever realizing the horrors surrounding them.

These creatures were born from the negative emotions of humans. Fear, anger, hatred, and regret gave them power, allowing them to haunt places where people gathered and turn ordinary moments into terrifying nightmares.

Only a small number of people could see these Curses. They were known as Jujutsu Sorcerers, individuals trained to fight supernatural threats using cursed energy.

But Yuji Itadori had no idea that his life was about to become connected to this hidden world.

Sometimes, one decision can change everything.

Yuji was an unusually athletic high school student. Despite possessing incredible physical strength, he preferred spending his time with the members of his school's Occult Research Club rather than joining the athletics team.

His grandfather, Wasuke Itadori, was one of the most important people in his life. Before his passing, his grandfather left Yuji with words that would stay with him long after that day.

He encouraged Yuji to help others and to make sure that he would not face the end of his life alone.

"Help others whenever you can."

Yuji did not fully understand how much those words would influence his future. He only knew that he wanted to live a life in which his actions could make a difference.

Meanwhile, the members of the Occult Research Club had discovered a mysterious object sealed inside a container. Unbeknownst to them, it was a dangerous cursed object: one of the fingers of Ryomen Sukuna, a legendary and immensely powerful Curse known as the King of Curses.

The finger carried an overwhelming presence. Its seal protected those nearby from the danger it contained, but that protection would not last forever.

RYOMEN SUKUNA — THE KING OF CURSES

At Jujutsu High, Megumi Fushiguro was searching for the missing cursed object. He understood how dangerous it could be if the seal was removed and the object fell into the wrong hands.

His search eventually led him to Yuji and the Occult Research Club. But by the time he arrived, the situation had already begun to spiral out of control.

The club members had removed the seal, unknowingly attracting nearby Curses. What had started as an ordinary school activity suddenly became a fight for survival.

Megumi rushed to protect the students, but the Curses proved to be more dangerous than expected. Yuji realized that his friends were in immediate danger and refused to stand by while they were attacked.

He had no formal training in Jujutsu, no experience fighting Curses, and no understanding of the power contained within the object.

Yet he could not simply abandon the people who mattered to him.

"I can't just leave them behind!"

Faced with a desperate situation, Yuji made a decision that would change the course of his life. He swallowed Sukuna's finger, taking the cursed object into his own body in order to gain the power needed to fight back.

It was an extraordinary and dangerous act. The finger belonged to a being whose power had terrified the Jujutsu world for generations.

For a moment, everything seemed lost. The presence of Ryomen Sukuna emerged, bringing with it an overwhelming sense of danger and destruction.

The King of Curses had found a vessel.

But something unexpected happened. Yuji retained control over his own body, resisting Sukuna's influence in a way that few could have imagined possible.

His incredible ability to contain Sukuna made him both an extraordinary discovery and a serious concern for the Jujutsu world.

Before long, Satoru Gojo, a powerful Jujutsu Sorcerer and teacher at Tokyo Jujutsu High, became involved in Yuji's situation.

Yuji's ordinary school life was over. He had stepped into a world of Curses, dangerous secrets, and battles far beyond anything he had ever experienced.

The decision to save his friends had connected him to one of the most feared beings in history.

He still had much to learn about cursed energy, Jujutsu Sorcerers, and the burden he now carried.

But Yuji had already shown the quality that would define his journey: the willingness to risk himself to protect others.

And so, Yuji Itadori's journey into the world of Jujutsu Sorcery began.
