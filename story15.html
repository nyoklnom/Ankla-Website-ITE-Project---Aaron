Dragon Ball Super

by Aaron

Dragon Ball Super

Chapter 1

After the fierce battle against Majin Buu, peace finally returns to Earth. Son Goku spends his days working on a farm, but his heart still longs for powerful opponents and exciting battles. Although his family wants him to enjoy a quieter life, Goku can never completely abandon his dream of becoming stronger.

Meanwhile, far beyond Earth, a powerful being named Beerus, the God of Destruction, awakens from a long sleep. After dreaming of a mysterious warrior known as the Super Saiyan God, Beerus begins searching the universe for an opponent worthy of his strength. His search eventually leads him toward Goku, whose growing power attracts the attention of the divine world.

Goku's desire to become stronger takes him beyond Earth and into a universe filled with gods, powerful warriors, and unimaginable challenges.

When Beerus arrives on Earth, Goku eagerly challenges him, hoping to test his own limits. However, the difference in their strength becomes clear almost immediately. With the help of his friends and the power of the Super Saiyan God, Goku manages to experience a level of power he has never known before. Even after the battle ends, he realizes that an entire world of stronger opponents awaits him.

Goku's journey continues as he trains under Whis, Beerus's mysterious attendant, alongside Vegeta. Whis teaches them to improve their techniques, control their energy, and remain calm even when facing overwhelming enemies. Vegeta, determined not to fall behind Goku, pushes himself through intense training, turning their rivalry into a powerful source of motivation.

Soon, the warriors face new threats and challenges. Frieza returns with a desire for revenge, forcing Goku and Vegeta to unlock even greater power. Their battles reveal new transformations and test how far their training has taken them. Yet every victory brings another reminder that the universe is much larger and more dangerous than they imagined.

THE BATTLE BEYOND THE UNIVERSE

The arrival of Champa, Beerus's counterpart from Universe 6, introduces a tournament between powerful fighters from different universes. Goku and Vegeta join the competition, where they encounter warriors with unfamiliar abilities and fighting styles. Each match challenges them to adapt, think quickly, and discover new ways to use their strength.

Their adventures grow even more dangerous when a mysterious enemy known as Goku Black appears. Bearing Goku's face but possessing a cruel and destructive personality, this opponent threatens the future of Trunks's world. As the mystery unfolds, the heroes confront a terrifying plan that places countless lives at risk.

Just when the warriors believe they have experienced the greatest battles imaginable, Zeno, the supreme ruler of the multiverse, announces the Tournament of Power. Fighters from multiple universes must compete in a massive battle for survival, where losing a match means the erasure of their entire universe. Goku gathers Earth's strongest warriors, including Vegeta, Gohan, Piccolo, Android 17, and Frieza, to represent Universe 7.

Inside the arena, alliances form and collapse as fighters struggle against opponents with incredible speed, strength, and unique techniques. Goku faces Jiren, an extraordinarily powerful warrior whose strength challenges everything Goku understands about combat. Pushed beyond his limits, Goku approaches a remarkable state of battle known as Ultra Instinct, allowing his body to react with extraordinary speed and precision.

The tournament becomes a test of more than individual power. Trust, teamwork, determination, and sacrifice become essential as Universe 7 fights to survive. Even the strongest warriors must learn that victory cannot always be achieved alone, especially when the fate of entire worlds rests on the outcome.

From facing gods to fighting across the multiverse, Goku and his friends continue pushing beyond every limit they encounter. Dragon Ball Super follows their endless pursuit of strength, the rivalries that inspire them, and the friendships that keep them fighting. For Goku, every new challenge is another opportunity to grow stronger and discover just how far his power can truly reach.
