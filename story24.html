My Hero Academia: The Making of Deku

by Aaron

My Hero Academia: The Making of Deku

Chapter 1

In a society where most people are born with extraordinary abilities known as Quirks, Izuku Midoriya grows up as an ordinary boy with an extraordinary dream. He wants to become a professional hero like All Might, the Symbol of Peace, who inspires millions with his courage and reassuring smile. However, Izuku is born without a Quirk, making his dream seem impossible in a world where superpowers determine much of a person's future.

Despite being mocked and discouraged, Izuku never stops studying heroes. He fills notebooks with observations about their abilities, weaknesses, and fighting techniques. While others see his lack of power as a reason to give up, Izuku continues believing that saving people is what truly makes someone a hero.

Before Izuku ever receives a Quirk, he already possesses the courage to run toward danger when someone needs help.

One day, Izuku's life changes when he encounters All Might, who reveals that even the greatest hero has weaknesses and limitations. After witnessing Izuku's selfless attempt to help his childhood rival, Katsuki Bakugo, All Might recognizes something special in the boy. He decides that Izuku has the heart of a true hero and offers him the chance to inherit his powerful Quirk, One For All.

Accepting this opportunity means preparing his body for a power he has never experienced before. All Might creates an intense training program to help Izuku build the strength and endurance necessary to handle One For All. Through months of exhausting work, Izuku learns that receiving great power is only the beginning. He must earn the ability to control it.

THE FIRST STEP TOWARD GREATNESS

After successfully inheriting One For All, Izuku enters U.A. High School, a prestigious academy dedicated to training future heroes. His new classmates possess a wide variety of Quirks, from explosive attacks to ice and fire abilities. Surrounded by talented students, Izuku realizes that he has a long way to go before he can stand among the heroes he admires.

His first major challenge is learning to use One For All without destroying his own body. The Quirk contains immense strength, but Izuku initially struggles to control even a fraction of its power. During training exercises, he must learn to think quickly, analyze situations, and find creative solutions instead of relying entirely on brute force.

At U.A., Izuku develops friendships with classmates such as Ochaco Uraraka and Tenya Iida, who support his growth in different ways. His relationship with Bakugo remains complicated, as their childhood rivalry becomes more intense now that both are pursuing the same goal. Their competition forces Izuku to confront his insecurities and recognize that becoming stronger requires honest effort.

Under the guidance of his teachers, especially Shota Aizawa and All Might, Izuku begins developing his own fighting style. He learns to use his intelligence as an advantage, studying opponents and adapting when his original plans fail. Every mistake becomes another lesson, helping him understand the responsibilities that come with becoming a professional hero.

THE HERO DEKU BECOMES

When dangerous villains threaten the students and the people they are supposed to protect, Izuku discovers that classroom exercises cannot fully prepare anyone for real danger. Fear, uncertainty, and the possibility of failure become unavoidable. Nevertheless, he continues stepping forward whenever someone needs help, even when his own safety is at risk.

As he gains experience, Izuku gradually improves his control over One For All and begins exploring new ways to apply its power. He learns from professional heroes, studies his classmates, and pushes himself through increasingly difficult challenges. His progress is not always smooth, but every setback teaches him something important about his abilities and his limits.

More importantly, Izuku begins understanding that he cannot become All Might simply by copying his actions. He must discover what kind of hero he wants to be. His compassion, careful analysis, and willingness to risk himself for others become the foundation of his own identity as a hero.

Izuku Midoriya's transformation from a Quirkless boy into a determined hero-in-training proves that greatness is not achieved through power alone. It is built through sacrifice, perseverance, compassion, and the courage to keep moving forward after failure. Although the road ahead remains difficult, Deku continues working toward his dream, determined to become a hero who can save people with a reassuring smile and give others the same hope that All Might once gave him.
