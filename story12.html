Fire Force

by Aaron

Fire Force

Chapter 1

Across the vast oceans of the Grand Line, where pirates chase dreams and the World Government struggles to maintain order, one name burns as brightly as a flame: Portgas D. Ace. Known as Fire Fist Ace, he is a powerful pirate whose ability to create and control fire makes him a fearsome opponent. With the power of the Flame-Flame Fruit, Ace can transform his body into fire and unleash devastating attacks against anyone who threatens him.

Ace grew up alongside his sworn brothers, Monkey D. Luffy and Sabo. Although they came from different backgrounds, the three formed an unbreakable bond through their adventures, rivalries, and shared dreams. They promised to live freely and become strong enough to follow their own paths across the world.

Ace's greatest treasure was not his strength or his reputation as a pirate, but the people he considered his family.

As he grew older, Ace sailed across the seas and eventually became the commander of the Whitebeard Pirates' Second Division. Under the protection of Edward Newgate, known as Whitebeard, Ace found a place where he could belong. He came to respect Whitebeard as a father and developed a deep loyalty to the crew that accepted him.

His fiery abilities made him famous throughout the pirate world. With a single movement, Ace could unleash enormous bursts of flame, surround himself in blazing energy, and overwhelm enemies with attacks that turned the battlefield into a sea of fire. Yet beneath his confident personality was a man who often questioned his own worth and whether he truly deserved to exist.

THE FLAME THAT WOULD NEVER DIE

Ace's journey took a dangerous turn when he pursued Blackbeard, a former member of Whitebeard's crew who had committed a terrible betrayal. Determined to bring him to justice, Ace refused to abandon the hunt, unaware of how deeply the confrontation would affect the balance of power across the world.

Their clash ended with Ace captured and sentenced to execution by the Marines. The news spread across the seas, drawing powerful pirates and legendary warriors toward Marineford. Whitebeard and his crew launched a desperate rescue, determined to save the man they considered their family.

The battlefield became a nightmare of explosions, shattered ice, and blazing attacks. Luffy fought through countless enemies to reach his brother, refusing to give up despite the overwhelming odds. For a brief moment, hope seemed within reach as Ace was finally freed from his chains.

But their escape came at a terrible cost. Ace turned back to protect Luffy from a deadly attack, placing his brother's life above his own. In that moment, he showed the courage and love that had defined his life, proving that his strength was never measured by his flames alone.

Portgas D. Ace's journey became one of the most unforgettable tragedies in the pirate world. Though his life ended at Marineford, his courage, loyalty, and fiery spirit continued to inspire Luffy and everyone who had known him. His flames disappeared from the battlefield, but the memory of Fire Fist Ace would continue to burn in the hearts of those he left behind.
