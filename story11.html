Yamada-kun and the Seven Witches

by Aaron

Yamada-kun and the Seven Witches

Chapter 1

At Suzaku High School, Ryu Yamada had earned a reputation as a troublesome student. He often skipped classes, got into fights, and showed little interest in his schoolwork. Many of his classmates believed he was simply a delinquent with no plans for his future. However, beneath his rough exterior was someone who had not yet found a reason to care about school or the people around him.

Everything began to change when Yamada accidentally collided with Urara Shiraishi, one of the school's top students. Shiraishi was intelligent, hardworking, and respected by her teachers, making her the complete opposite of Yamada. Their lives seemed to have nothing in common, but a strange accident would connect them in a way neither could have imagined.

After falling down the stairs together, Yamada and Shiraishi discovered something unbelievable: they had switched bodies. Yamada suddenly found himself living inside Shiraishi's body, while Shiraishi experienced life through his. At first, the situation caused confusion, embarrassment, and plenty of unexpected problems at school.

One unexpected kiss would reveal a secret that changed their school lives forever.

After experimenting with their strange condition, they realized that kissing could trigger the body swap. What seemed like an impossible accident was actually connected to a mysterious supernatural ability. Their discovery raised even more questions: why did they possess this power, and were there other students hiding similar secrets?

As Yamada and Shiraishi tried to understand what was happening, they became involved with the school's Supernatural Studies Club. With help from other students, they began investigating unusual abilities and searching for the truth behind the strange events occurring around Suzaku High.

Yamada soon learned that Shiraishi was not simply a brilliant student. She also struggled with loneliness and the feeling that other people kept their distance because of her intelligence. Spending time together allowed Yamada to understand her more deeply, while Shiraishi began seeing a different side of the boy everyone had dismissed as a delinquent.

THE MYSTERY OF THE SEVEN WITCHES

Their investigation uncovered rumors about seven female students at the school who possessed mysterious supernatural powers. Each witch had a different ability, and these powers could influence the lives of students in unexpected ways. The secret behind the witches appeared to be connected to the strange phenomenon Yamada and Shiraishi had experienced.

To uncover the truth, Yamada had to interact with students he barely knew and confront situations far stranger than anything he had encountered before. His ability to copy a witch's power through a kiss made him an important part of the investigation, although it also created awkward misunderstandings and complicated relationships.

Along the way, Yamada met Toranosuke Miyamura, a student who helped him become involved in the Supernatural Studies Club. He also encountered Miyabi Itou, who was fascinated by supernatural mysteries, and several other students whose personalities and secrets made every discovery more complicated than the last.

Behind every mysterious power was a person with their own fears, wishes, and secrets.

As the group investigated the witches, Yamada began to realize that supernatural abilities were not merely amusing tricks. They could change friendships, influence decisions, and expose feelings that people had tried to hide. Understanding each witch required more than discovering her power; it meant learning about the person behind it.

Shiraishi gradually became closer to Yamada as they faced these strange challenges together. Their relationship grew from an awkward partnership into a genuine connection built on trust and understanding. Although Yamada often acted impulsively, he repeatedly showed that he cared about protecting the people who mattered to him.

However, the deeper they went into the mystery, the more complicated the situation became. Different students had their own reasons for keeping secrets, and not everyone wanted the witches' powers to be understood. The club's discoveries threatened to uncover truths that could change the school and the relationships between its students.

Yamada, who once wanted nothing to do with school, now found himself surrounded by friends, mysteries, and responsibilities. His unusual ability forced him to look beyond appearances and recognize that everyone carried a story others might never see.

What began with a simple collision between two students became a supernatural adventure filled with comedy, romance, unexpected friendships, and secrets hidden within the school walls. Every new discovery brought Yamada closer to understanding the seven witches and the strange powers connecting them.

At Suzaku High School, a kiss could reveal a supernatural secret, change two people's lives, or bring hidden feelings to the surface. As Yamada and his friends continued their investigation, the mystery of the seven witches promised to transform their school days in ways none of them could have imagined.
